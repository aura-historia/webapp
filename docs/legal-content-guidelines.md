# Legal Content Guidelines

Apply this guide to terms, imprint, and privacy content in every locale.

## Source And Meaning

- Treat the German legal text as the primary source for German-law meaning.
- Keep every locale semantically aligned with the approved source; translate legal meaning, not marketing tone.
- Preserve headings and the legal structure across locales.

## Do Not Invent

- Do not add guarantees, compliance claims, processor relationships, retention periods, or data-subject rights workflows without approved source material.
- Do not alter legal obligations, user rights, or data-processing claims through translation.
- Flag ambiguity or a needed legal change for legal/product review before publishing.

## Provider Attribution And Dates

- Keep the conspicuous [Amazon Location Service attribution](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/location/latest/developerguide/data-attribution.html) link in the privacy policy and terms for every locale while retained geocoding is in use.
- Update the `Stand`/status date in every locale of a document whenever its content changes.
- Keep section numbering identical across locales; tests check the privacy policy sections.
